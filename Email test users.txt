Hi [name],

I'd love you to try out Canopy Family, an app I've been building for families who share parenting across two homes.

To get started:

1. On your iPhone, install the TestFlight app from the App Store (it's free, made by Apple)
2. Check your email for an invite from Apple — it'll come from noreply@email.apple.com
3. Open the invite on your iPhone and tap Accept
4. TestFlight will let you install Canopy Family directly

What to test:
- Sign up and create a family
- Add a second parent (they'll get an invite email)
- Add some events to the calendar and check they appear correctly
- Try the notice board — post an update, share a document
- Schools — add your child's school via Settings → Schools
- Term dates — once a school is added, check that term dates import and appear in the calendar
- FamilyFeed — go to Settings → FamilyFeed, enable it, then forward a school newsletter or appointment letter to familyfeed@canopy-app.app and see if it extracts events automatically
- Direct messaging — send a message to the other parent
- Expenses — log a shared expense and check it appears for both parents

What I'd love feedback on:
- Anything that felt confusing or didn't work as expected
- Anything that's missing that you'd want
- How it feels to use day to day — is it clear what everything does?

Just reply to this message with anything you notice. All feedback welcome — nothing is too small.
