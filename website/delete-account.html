Account deletion

Delete your account

You can permanently delete your Canopy account and all associated data at any time — from within the app, or by request if you no longer have access to it.

Option 1: Delete it yourself (instant)

  1. Sign in at my.canopy-app.app (in a browser, or the Canopy app)
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Scroll to Account and select Delete account
  4. Confirm — your account is deleted immediately
Go to Settings

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can no longer sign in, email hello@canopy-app.app from the address associated with your account and ask us to delete it. We'll verify your identity and process the request within 30 days, in line with UK GDPR.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Your profile, calendar, notices, messages, and Info Bank entries you've added
  • Documents you've uploaded to the vault
  • If you're the last remaining parent in a family: the whole family's data is deleted too, including shared documents and uploaded files
  • If a co-parent remains: only your own membership and personal data are removed — shared family data (calendar, schedule, notices) stays intact for them

Deletion is immediate and permanent once confirmed — it can't be undone, and there's no recovery period.
