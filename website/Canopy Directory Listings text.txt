Hi Product Hunt — I built Canopy after seeing how much of co-parenting life gets lost across group chats, paper calendars, and a dozen different apps. Canopy pulls it into one private place: a shared custody calendar that handles school term dates automatically (AI reads forwarded school emails and adds events for you), a notice board, a secure document vault for things like passports and court orders, and expense tracking.
