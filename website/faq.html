🔐 Security & Privacy

Yes. Canopy is built with security as a first principle, not an afterthought. Here’s what that means in practice:

  • ✓ All data travels over HTTPS — it is encrypted in transit.
  • ✓ Everything stored in our database is encrypted at rest using AES-256.
  • ✓ Passwords are hashed using bcrypt and never stored in plain text.
  • ✓ Row-level security means only authenticated members of your family can ever read your family’s data. Our staff cannot query your records.
  • ✓ Our infrastructure runs in the UK (London), and we process data lawfully wherever you and your family are based.

In the UK. Your family’s data — the database and every file in the Document Vault — is hosted on infrastructure in London, England, in a single region. This applies no matter which country you or your co-parent live in, including Ireland, Australia, the US, and New Zealand.

We keep everything in one region rather than splitting it by country, which is what allows us to enforce row-level security and back up your data consistently. Where this means personal data is transferred outside your home country, we put appropriate legal safeguards in place — Standard Contractual Clauses and/or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, depending on where you’re based. Full detail is in Section 6 of our Privacy Policy.

No system can guarantee zero risk, but Canopy uses the same infrastructure and security model trusted by thousands of enterprise applications worldwide. Specifically:

  • ✓ Row-level security (RLS) is enforced at the database level — even if someone obtained your API keys, they could not read another family’s data.
  • ✓ Two-factor authentication is available in Settings. We strongly recommend enabling it — even if your password is ever compromised, a second factor stops an attacker from signing in.
  • ✓ Session management — you can sign out of all devices from Settings if you ever suspect unauthorised access.
  • ✓ Child account credentials are stored with an additional encryption layer on top of standard password hashing.

As with any online service, using a strong, unique password and enabling two-factor authentication are the two most effective steps you can take.

Never. Canopy is funded entirely by subscriptions. There are no adverts in the app, no third-party advertising trackers, and we do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone for commercial purposes.

You have the right to access, correct, port, and erase your data — and in Canopy, you can exercise all of these rights directly in the app without contacting us. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Your Documents

Passports, school reports, medical letters — the most sensitive files you own. Here’s exactly how we protect them.

Very safe. Here is the full picture:

🔒

Encrypted at rest

Every file you upload is stored in Supabase Storage with AES-256 encryption at rest. The encryption is applied at the storage layer — files are never stored as raw bytes that could be read if the underlying disk were accessed.

🔐

Only your family can access them

Row-level security is enforced at the database layer — not just in application code. This means even a malicious actor who bypassed the application layer would still be blocked from reading files belonging to other families. Access is limited to authenticated members of your family group.

🔗

Signed, expiring download links

When you view or download a document, Canopy generates a signed URL that expires after a short period. This means there is no permanent, guessable link to your files. Bookmarking a download link will not work after it expires.

🚫

We cannot read your documents

Canopy staff do not have routine access to your stored files. Our infrastructure access controls are set so that files can only be accessed by authenticated users who belong to the relevant family. We do not process document contents with AI or for any purpose other than storing and serving them back to you.

🇬🇧

Data stays in the UK

All storage is hosted in the UK (London). Your documents never leave without your knowledge, and we process data lawfully wherever you and your family are based.

The Document Vault is designed specifically for sensitive family documents — passports, medical letters, school reports. We take its security proportionally seriously.

By default, both parents can view and upload documents. If you add a read-only family member (for example, a grandparent or carer), access to the Info Bank — which includes documents — is off by default. Both parents must agree to grant them access.

Read-only members never have permission to upload, edit, or delete documents — they can only view files that both parents have explicitly granted them access to see.

Any file type is supported. Common uses include:

  • Passports and ID documents
  • School reports and letters
  • Medical letters, test results, prescriptions
  • Court orders and parenting agreements
  • Insurance documents
  • Travel documents and consent forms

📅 How to import your Canopy calendar

Step-by-step guides for Google Calendar and Outlook. Your iCal link is in Settings → Calendar → Export inside the Canopy app.

Google Calendar

1

Copy your Canopy iCal link

In the Canopy app, go to Settings → Calendar → Export and tap Copy link.

2

Open “Other calendars” in Google Calendar

On the left sidebar, find Other calendars and click the + button next to it. Select From URL.

3

Paste your Canopy link and add the calendar

Paste the link you copied from Canopy into the URL of calendar field, then click Add calendar.

4

Done — your Canopy calendar appears

Your family calendar events will appear in Google Calendar and update automatically. Google typically refreshes subscribed calendars every few hours.

Microsoft Outlook

These steps work for Outlook on the web (outlook.com / Microsoft 365) and the Outlook desktop app.

1

Copy your Canopy iCal link

In the Canopy app, go to Settings → Calendar → Export and tap Copy link.

2

Open Calendar and click “Add calendar”

Switch to the Calendar view in Outlook. In the left panel, click Add calendar (or the + icon).

3

Choose “Subscribe from web”

In the dialog that opens, select Subscribe from web.

4

Paste your Canopy link and import

Paste your Canopy iCal URL into the field and click Import. You can give it a name like “Canopy Family”.

5

Done — your Canopy calendar appears

Your family events will appear alongside your work calendar in Outlook. Outlook refreshes subscribed calendars periodically — typically every few hours.

📅

Apple Calendar (Mac)

1

Copy your Canopy iCal link from Settings → Calendar → Export

2

Open Calendar on your Mac, go to File → New Calendar Subscription

3

Paste the link, set refresh to Every 15 minutes, click OK

Enabling iCloud sync means the subscription will also appear on your iPhone and iPad.

Note: The iCal feed is read-only. Events you add in Google, Outlook, or Apple Calendar will not appear in Canopy. Canopy is the source of truth for your family calendar.
