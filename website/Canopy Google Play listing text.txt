Family & parenting calendar, schedules, notices — all in one private app


FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 char limit)
----------------------------------
Canopy is a private family app for staying organised together — shared parenting schedules, school updates, notices, and everything important about your kids, all in one secure place.

It works for any family — parenting, co-parenting, or parallel parenting. Some features are especially useful for co-parents and parallel parenting families — shared custody schedules, First Right of Refusal, and expense splitting — alongside everything every family needs: a notice board, school term dates, document storage, and more.

SHARED CALENDAR & PARENTING SCHEDULE
Set your rotation (alternating weeks, 2-2-5-5, 2-2-3, or fully custom) and the calendar updates itself. See who has the children on any day at a glance. Request schedule changes or holiday time, and First Right of Refusal offers cover to the other parent first when needed. Subscribe from Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook.

SCHOOL EMAILS, HANDLED AUTOMATICALLY
Forward school newsletters to your family's own Canopy email address. AI reads them and adds events, term dates, and appointments straight to your calendar.

TERM DATES, ALWAYS UP TO DATE
Add your child's school once and holidays, half-terms, and INSET days stay synced automatically.

FAMILY NOTICE BOARD
Post updates, photos, and reminders, tagged by topic. Everyone's notified the moment something's posted.

CHILDREN'S INFO, ALL IN ONE PLACE
Medical details, school contacts, GP/dentist info, accounts, and documents, organised per child.

SECURE DOCUMENT VAULT
Passports, medical records, school reports, encrypted and always at hand.

SHARED EXPENSES & CHILDCARE
Split costs your way, track carer hours, roll them into bills automatically.

DIRECT MESSAGING
A private thread between parents.

VERIFIED FAMILY RECORDS
Export a tamper-proof, timestamped record of activity for legal or court proceedings if you ever need it — useful for co-parenting and custody schedule disputes.

READ-ONLY ACCESS FOR GRANDPARENTS & CARERS
Give trusted family members visibility without full access.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Encrypted, GDPR compliant, no ads, no data selling. AI features are strictly opt-in.

One plan covers the whole family, not a per-parent fee.
