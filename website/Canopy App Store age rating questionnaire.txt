CANOPY — APP STORE AGE RATING QUESTIONNAIRE (draft)
Drafted 2026-07-06. Apple's exact question wording in App Store Connect can drift
from what's below — treat this as "what to click," verify each question's live
text against the actual form before submitting, don't paste blind.

Fill this in at: App Store Connect > [Canopy Family app] > App Privacy's neighbour
tab, "Age Rating" > Set Age Rating

============================================================
CONTENT CATEGORIES — all "None" (no built-in content of these kinds anywhere
in the app; every one of these questions should be answered at the lowest/
"None" option)
============================================================
- Cartoon or Fantasy Violence — None
- Realistic Violence — None
- Sexual Content or Nudity — None
- Profanity or Crude Humor — None
- Alcohol, Tobacco, or Drug Use or References — None
- Mature or Suggestive Themes — None
- Horror or Fear Themes — None
- Gambling (Simulated) — None
- Medical/Treatment Information — None
  (Note: the app STORES health info a parent enters about their child in
  Info Bank, but doesn't provide medical advice/treatment content itself —

  personal data. Different from the privacy label's Health data type.)
- Contests — None
- Unrestricted Web Access — No (the app doesn't embed an open web browser;
  ConfigPage links out to canopy-app.app/privacy.html etc. via the device's own
  browser, which doesn't count as in-app unrestricted access)

============================================================
THE TWO THAT ACTUALLY APPLY
============================================================

User-Generated Content — YES
  Present in: notice board posts (text + image/file attachments), direct
  messages between family members, calendar event notes, FamilyFeed-derived
  content (originates from a forwarded school email, not another app user, but
  becomes visible to other family members same as any other post).

  Follow-up sub-questions Apple asks (2023+ format) and how to answer:
  - "Is content moderated?" — No automated moderation exists; content is only
    visible within one private family group, never public. Answer based on
    whichever option best matches "private, invite-only group, not moderated,
    not visible to the public or unknown users" — this is the option that
    typically keeps the rating low, as opposed to open/public UGC platforms.
  - "Can users interact with people they don't already know?" — No. Every
    person who can see a family's content is either a co-parent added via a
    family_invites code the account holder generated themselves, or a
    third_party (grandparent/carer) added the same way. There's no discovery,
    matching, or public feed — this is the key fact that distinguishes Canopy
    from a social app for rating purposes.

Communication/Messaging between users — YES, but closed-group only
  `messages`/`message_threads` (migration 025) are scoped to family_id via RLS
  — a user can only message people already in their own family, never an
  arbitrary stranger. Answer any "who can users communicate with" question as
  "only people they've already connected with in a private group," not
  "anyone"/"the public."

============================================================
EXPECTED OUTCOME
============================================================
With every content category at None, and the only Yes answers being
closed-group UGC/messaging with no stranger contact and no public exposure,
this should land at the lowest or near-lowest tier Apple offers (historically
4+ for this content profile; Apple's exact tier labels/thresholds are set by
their live form, not fixed — read what the form actually shows once these
answers are entered, don't assume the number in advance).
